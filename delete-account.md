# PowerME — Delete Your Account and Data

**Last updated:** 2026-08-05

---

## 1. Delete From Within the App

You can delete your PowerME account and all associated data at any time, directly in the app:

1. Open **PowerME** and sign in.
2. Go to the **Profile** screen.
3. Scroll to **Danger Zone**.
4. Tap **Delete Account** and confirm.

For your security, you may be asked to sign in again before the deletion is carried out.

Deletion is **immediate and irreversible**.

---

## 2. Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app — for example, you have lost access to your sign-in email — you can request deletion by contacting us.

Email **powerme.support@gmail.com** from the email address registered to your PowerME account, with the subject line **"Delete my account"**.

We will verify that the request comes from the account owner and complete the deletion within **30 days** of the verified request.

---

## 3. What Is Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes everything stored under your account:

- Your **account** and sign-in credentials (Firebase Auth user record)
- **Profile details** — name, date of birth, gender, height, weight, body fat percentage, training goals, experience level and schedule preferences
- **All training data** — workouts, exercises, sets, routines, templates and workout history
- **Health and injury history** you entered, including any injury notes and derived restrictions
- **Body and activity metrics** imported into PowerME from Health Connect
- **App settings and preferences** stored against your account

Data held on your device is also wiped when you delete your account or sign out.

---

## 4. What Is Not Deleted

- **Your Health Connect data is not affected.** PowerME reads body and activity data from Health Connect but does not own it. Those records remain in Health Connect and are yours to manage — use the **Health Connect** app on your device to view or delete them.
- **Workouts PowerME wrote to Health Connect remain in Health Connect.** If you allowed PowerME to save completed workouts to Health Connect, those exercise sessions stay there after your PowerME account is deleted. Remove them from the Health Connect app if you wish.
- **Crash reports** are retained for **90 days**, per Firebase's standard policy.
- **Analytics data** is aggregated and anonymised and expires per Firebase's standard retention policy. It is not linked to your identity and cannot be used to identify you.
