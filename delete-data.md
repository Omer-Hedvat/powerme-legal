# PowerME — Delete Your Data

**App:** PowerME (Android)
**Developer:** Omer Hedvat
**Contact:** powerme.support@gmail.com
**Last updated:** 2026-08-05

This page explains how to delete your data in **PowerME** without deleting your account. If you want to delete your account entirely, see [Delete Your Account](delete-account).

---

## 1. Delete Data Yourself, In the App

You can delete individual items at any time. Your account stays active and everything else is untouched. Deletions are applied to our servers, not just your phone.

| What you want to remove | Steps |
|---|---|
| **A workout session** | **History** tab → tap the workout → menu → **Delete** |
| **A routine or template** | **Workouts** tab → menu on the routine → **Delete** |
| **A custom exercise** | **Exercises** tab → open the exercise → **Delete** |
| **Imported workout history (CSV)** | **History** tab → select the imported entry → **Delete** |
| **An injury or health entry** | **Profile** → **Health History** → open the entry → **Delete** |

Deleting a workout removes all of its sets. To remove anything not listed above, use the email request below.

---

## 2. Request Bulk Deletion by Email

To have an entire category of data removed at once — for example *all* health and injury history, or *all* workout history — email us and say what you want deleted.

**Email:** **powerme.support@gmail.com**
**Subject:** "Delete my data"
**Include:** the email address registered to your PowerME account, and which categories you want removed.

We will verify the request comes from the account owner and complete the deletion within **30 days**.

---

## 3. What Is Deleted

When you delete an item, or ask us to delete a category, we permanently remove it from our servers (Firebase Firestore) and from your device:

- Workouts, sets, exercises, routines and templates
- Health and injury history, including derived exercise restrictions
- Body metrics — weight, body fat percentage, height
- Profile details — name, date of birth, gender, training goals, experience level, schedule preferences
- App settings and preferences

---

## 4. What Is Kept

- **Your account remains active** unless you also delete it. Your sign-in credentials are not affected by data deletion.
- **Health Connect data is not affected.** PowerME reads body and activity data from Health Connect but does not own it. Those records stay in Health Connect — manage or delete them in the **Health Connect** app on your device.
- **Workouts PowerME saved to Health Connect stay in Health Connect.** If you allowed PowerME to write completed workouts there, deleting them in PowerME does not remove them from Health Connect. Delete them from the Health Connect app.
- **Crash reports** are kept for **90 days**, per Firebase's standard policy. They are diagnostic and are not used to identify you.
- **Analytics data** is aggregated and anonymised, and expires per Firebase's standard retention policy. It is not linked to your identity.
